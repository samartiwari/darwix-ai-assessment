# Arogya First — Customer FAQ Sheet

Version 1.9 | Last updated 15-03-2025 | Contact centre reference

## What is a waiting period?

A waiting period is the time you must hold the policy before a particular
benefit becomes claimable. Arogya First applies an initial cooling period of 30
days from policy start, except for accidental hospitalisation which is covered
immediately.

## When are pre-existing conditions covered?

Pre-existing diseases are covered after 24 months of continuous coverage under
the policy. Any condition diagnosed or treated in the 48 months before the
policy start date is treated as pre-existing.

## What is the waiting period for specified illnesses?

Specified illnesses including cataract, hernia, joint replacement and benign
prostate conditions have a cooling period of 24 months from policy start.

## What is the initial waiting period?

The initial waiting period is 30 days from the policy start date. Accidental
hospitalisation is covered from day one.

## How large is the cashless hospital network?

Arogya First has a cashless network of 8,700 hospitals across 480 cities.
Pre-authorisation for cashless treatment is decided within 60 minutes once
complete documents are received.

## How long does a reimbursement claim take?

Reimbursement claims are settled within 15 working days of receiving complete
documents. Incomplete documentation is the most common cause of delay.

## Can I cover my parents on the same policy?

Dependent parents can be added to an Arogya First Family Floater. If a parent is
above 60, Senior Care is usually the better option because the floater's shared
coverage limit can be exhausted by a single senior claim.

## What is restoration of cover?

If your coverage limit is exhausted by a claim, restoration reinstates it once
within the policy year. For a cover amount of Rs 25 lakh or above, restoration
is unlimited within the policy year.

## Is maternity covered?

Maternity is covered under Family Floater after a 36 month waiting period,
limited to Rs 50,000 for a normal delivery and Rs 75,000 for a caesarean.
Newborn cover applies from day one once the maternity benefit is used.

## Does the policy cover treatment abroad?

Treatment outside India is excluded, except under Arogya First Secure Global.

## What tax benefit do I get?

Premium paid qualifies for deduction under section 80D of the Income Tax Act.
The limit is Rs 25,000 for self and family, and an additional Rs 50,000 where
premium is paid for parents aged 60 or above. Confirm your own position with a
tax adviser.

## What is a co-payment?

A co-payment is the share of an admissible claim you pay yourself. Senior Care
carries a 20% co-payment on every claim. The other products carry no
co-payment.

## What happens if I miss my renewal date?

A grace period of 30 days applies from the renewal due date. Cover is not
active during a lapse, and continuity of waiting periods is preserved only if
renewal is completed within the grace period.

## Can I port my existing policy to Arogya First?

Yes. Portability applications must be made at least 45 days before the renewal
date of the existing policy. Waiting periods already served with the previous
insurer are credited.

## Is a medical check-up needed?

A pre-policy medical check-up is mandatory for Senior Care, and for applicants
aged 46 to 60 seeking a cover amount above Rs 25 lakh.
