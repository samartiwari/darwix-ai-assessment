# Arogya First — Product Brochure

Version 2.1 | Effective 01/04/2025 | Internal distribution

Arogya First is a fictional insurer used for this prototype. Figures below are
illustrative and modelled on typical Indian retail health insurance terms.

## Arogya First Secure (individual)

Comprehensive indemnity cover for a single insured member.

- Cover amount options: Rs 5 lakh, Rs 10 lakh, Rs 25 lakh, Rs 50 lakh
- Entry age: 18 to 65 years
- Room rent: single private room, no capping
- Pre- and post-hospitalisation: 60 days before, 180 days after
- Day care procedures: all covered
- Restoration of cover amount: once per policy year, unlimited for cover
  amounts of Rs 25 lakh and above
- No claim bonus: 10% of cover amount per claim-free year, up to 50%
- Annual premium, 30 year old, Rs 10 lakh cover, Delhi: Rs 9,400
- Annual premium, 45 year old, Rs 10 lakh cover, Delhi: Rs 14,900

## Arogya First Family Floater

A single cover amount shared across the family.

- Members: self, spouse, up to three dependent children, and optionally
  dependent parents
- Cover amount options: Rs 10 lakh, Rs 25 lakh, Rs 50 lakh
- Entry age: 18 to 65 years for adults, 91 days to 25 years for children
- Cover amount is shared, not per member
- Maternity: covered after a 36 month waiting period, limited to Rs 50,000
  for normal delivery and Rs 75,000 for caesarean
- Newborn cover: from day one once maternity benefit is used
- Annual premium, family of four, eldest 35, Rs 10 lakh cover, Delhi: Rs 21,600

## Arogya First Senior Care

For applicants above 60 years.

- Cover amount options: Rs 5 lakh, Rs 10 lakh
- Entry age: 61 to 80 years
- Co-payment: 20% on every claim
- Pre-policy medical check-up: mandatory
- Room rent: capped at 1% of cover amount per day
- Domiciliary hospitalisation: covered up to Rs 50,000
- Annual premium, 65 year old, Rs 5 lakh cover, Delhi: Rs 28,300

## Arogya First Secure Top-up

Sits above an existing policy or employer cover.

- Deductible options: Rs 3 lakh, Rs 5 lakh, Rs 10 lakh
- Cover amount options: Rs 20 lakh, Rs 50 lakh, Rs 1 crore
- Entry age: 18 to 70 years
- Deductible applies per policy year, not per claim
- Annual premium, 35 year old, Rs 50 lakh cover over Rs 5 lakh deductible: Rs 6,800

## Waiting periods (all products)

- Initial waiting period: 30 days from policy start, accidents excepted
- Pre-existing disease waiting period: 36 months of continuous cover
- Specified illness waiting period: 24 months, covering cataract, hernia,
  joint replacement, and benign prostate conditions
- Maternity waiting period: 36 months

## Permanent exclusions

Cosmetic surgery unless reconstructive after accident or cancer; self-inflicted
injury; treatment for alcohol or substance abuse; unproven treatments;
participation in hazardous sports professionally; war and nuclear risk;
treatment taken outside India, except under Secure Global.

## Network and claims

- Cashless network: 8,700 hospitals across 480 cities
- Cashless pre-authorisation: decided within 60 minutes of complete documents
- Reimbursement settlement: within 15 working days of complete documents
- Claim intimation: within 24 hours for emergency admission, 48 hours before
  planned admission
