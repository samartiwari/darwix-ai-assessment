# Kalinga Life — Product and Policy Reference (Philippines)

Version 1.3 | Effective 2025-04-01 | Bancassurance and direct channel

Kalinga Life is a fictional Philippine life insurer used for this prototype.
Figures are illustrative and modelled on typical Philippine retail life and
bancassurance terms.

Terminology note: Philippine customers and agents use the English finance nouns
inside Filipino sentences — premium, policy, beneficiary, rider, lapse, coverage,
maturity, face amount. This document keeps them in English deliberately, because
translating them into pure Filipino would produce wording no customer or agent
actually says.

## Kalinga Protect (traditional whole life)

- Face amount: P500,000 / P1,000,000 / P2,000,000
- Entry age: 18 to 65 years old
- Premium paying period: 10 years, 20 years, or to age 65
- Monthly premium, 35 years old, P1,000,000 face amount: P2,480
- Monthly premium, 45 years old, P1,000,000 face amount: P3,940
- Payment modes: monthly, quarterly, semi-annual, annual
- Annual mode is discounted 5% against monthly mode
- Grace period: 31 days from the due date
- Beneficiary: primary and contingent may be named; revocable by default
- Policy loan: available after the policy has cash value, usually year 3

## Kalinga Bank Assure (bancassurance, single pay)

Sold through partner bank branches to deposit clients.

- Single premium: P50,000 minimum
- Face amount: 125% of single premium in year 1, rising to 150% by year 5
- Entry age: 18 to 70 years old
- No medical examination if the single premium is below P250,000
- Maturity: 10 years
- Bank referral: the branch relationship officer stays the servicing contact
- Withdrawal before year 5 carries a surrender charge of 3% of account value

## Kalinga Family Shield (term with riders)

- Face amount: P1,000,000 to P5,000,000
- Term: 10 or 20 years, renewable
- Entry age: 21 to 60 years old
- Monthly premium, 35 years old, P2,000,000 face amount, 20-year term: P1,250
- Available riders: critical illness, accidental death benefit, waiver of premium
  on total disability, hospital income
- Critical illness rider covers 36 listed conditions and pays 50% of face amount
  in advance of the death benefit

## Lapse and reinstatement

- A policy lapses when a premium remains unpaid after the 31-day grace period.
- Coverage is not active while a policy is lapsed. A claim during lapse is not
  payable.
- Reinstatement is possible within 3 years of the lapse date, subject to:
  payment of all overdue premiums with interest of 10% per year, and evidence of
  insurability.
- After 3 years the policy cannot be reinstated and a new application is needed
  at the customer's age at that time.
- Automatic premium loan: where the policy has sufficient cash value, an overdue
  premium can be advanced as a loan against the policy instead of lapsing. This
  must be requested; it is not applied automatically.

## Renewal and payment channels

- Due date reminders are sent 15 days and 3 days before the due date.
- Payment channels: partner bank branches, GCash, Maya, over-the-counter at
  payment centres, and auto-debit arrangement from a partner bank account.
- Auto-debit enrolment takes 1 billing cycle to take effect; the current due
  premium must still be paid manually.
- A payment posted through GCash reflects within 1 banking day.

## Claims

- Death claim requirements: claim form, original policy contract, death
  certificate from the Philippine Statistics Authority, and a valid government
  identification of the beneficiary.
- Claim decision: within 15 working days of complete documents.
- Contestability period: 2 years from issue or last reinstatement. A material
  misstatement discovered within this period can void the policy.
- Suicide within the first 2 years is excluded; premiums paid are returned.

## Free look and cancellation

- Free look period: 15 days from receipt of the policy. Full premium refund,
  less any medical examination cost already incurred.
- After free look, cancellation gives the surrender value, which for a
  traditional plan is nil in the first 2 years.

## Regulatory

- Kalinga Life is regulated by the Insurance Commission of the Philippines.
- Agents must state their licence number when asked.
- Complaints unresolved after 15 days may be brought to the Insurance
  Commission's Public Assistance and Mediation Division.
- Data collected is handled under the Data Privacy Act of 2012; a customer may
  request what data is held about them.
