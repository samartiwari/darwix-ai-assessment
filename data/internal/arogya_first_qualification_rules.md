# Arogya First — Lead Qualification Rules

Version 1.4 | Effective April 1, 2025 | Sales operations

These rules decide whether a lead proceeds to a quotation, needs underwriter
review, or is declined. The voice agent must not promise cover; it establishes
preliminary eligibility only.

## Required information before a quote

| Field | Requirement | If missing |
|---|---|---|
| Age of eldest member | Mandatory | Cannot quote, ask again |
| City / pin code | Mandatory | Cannot quote, affects zone pricing |
| Family members to cover | Mandatory | Cannot quote |
| Existing medical conditions | Mandatory, self-declared | Cannot quote |
| Annual budget | Optional | Quote all tiers |
| Existing cover | Optional | Recommend top-up if present |

## Age eligibility

| Age band | Eligible products | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 18 to 45 | Secure, Family Floater, Top-up | Standard underwriting |
| 46 to 60 | Secure, Family Floater, Top-up | Medical check-up if cover above Rs 25 lakh |
| 61 to 80 | Senior Care only | Pre-policy check-up mandatory, 20% co-payment |
| Above 80 | None | Decline politely, offer callback for group options |
| Below 18 | Dependent child only | Must be added to an adult policy |

## Zone pricing

| Zone | Cities | Premium loading |
|---|---|---|
| Zone A | Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai | Base rate |
| Zone B | Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad | Base less 10% |
| Zone C | All other cities | Base less 20% |

## Declared conditions

| Declared condition | Action |
|---|---|
| None | Proceed to quote |
| Diabetes or hypertension, controlled | Proceed, note 36 month PED waiting period |
| Thyroid disorder, asthma | Proceed, note 36 month PED waiting period |
| Cardiac history, stroke, cancer history | Underwriter review, no quote on call |
| Chronic kidney or liver disease | Underwriter review, no quote on call |
| Pregnancy at time of application | Cannot cover current pregnancy, explain maternity waiting period |
| Undisclosed conditions suspected | Warn that non-disclosure can void a claim |

## Budget guidance

| Annual budget | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Below Rs 8,000 | Top-up over employer cover, or Rs 5 lakh individual |
| Rs 8,000 to Rs 15,000 | Secure Rs 10 lakh individual |
| Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000 | Family Floater Rs 10 lakh |
| Above Rs 25,000 | Family Floater Rs 25 lakh or Secure Rs 25 lakh |

## Qualification outcome

- **Qualified** — all mandatory fields captured, age within an eligible band, no
  condition requiring underwriter review. Create lead, offer callback slot.
- **Referred** — declared condition needs underwriter review, or age above 80.
  Create lead flagged for a licensed advisor, do not quote.
- **Incomplete** — a mandatory field is missing or the caller gave conflicting
  details. Re-ask once; if still unresolved, create lead as incomplete and offer
  a callback.

## Escalation triggers

Transfer to a human advisor when the caller asks for a person, becomes upset,
raises a complaint about an existing policy, asks for medical advice, asks for
tax advice beyond the standard 80D statement, or requests something the agent
cannot confirm from approved material.

## Compliance requirements on every call

1. Identify as an automated assistant of Arogya First at the start.
2. State that the call is recorded for quality purposes.
3. Do not guarantee claim approval or promise cover for a specific condition.
4. Describe premiums as indicative and subject to underwriting.
5. Take verbal consent before creating a lead record.
